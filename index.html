Warehouse QC checklist

Compare photos of your item with the seller listing and the size and color you ordered. Check the agent’s return window before approving shipment.

Shoes

Compare both size labels and ask for an insole measurement if fit is uncertain. Inspect the toe shape, heel alignment, stitching, and sole attachment on both shoes.

Hoodies and T-shirts

Measure a garment you own across the chest and from shoulder to hem. Compare those measurements with the seller chart and request ruler photos if the listing is unclear.

Jackets

Compare sleeve length, check zipper alignment, and inspect the cuffs and seam finish. If you need waterproofing, check the seller’s specifications for the fabric and seams.

Estimate your total order cost

Add the item price, seller-to-warehouse delivery, any agent or payment fees, and international shipping. Include applicable import taxes and duties. Compare parcel quotes after packing dimensions are available because some shipping lines charge by volumetric weight.

When comparing two listings, write down the seller link, color, size, item price, and estimated shipping separately. A lower item price can come with a higher total cost if the parcel is heavier or bulkier.

Sugargoo spreadsheet FAQ

What is a Sugargoo spreadsheet?

It is a product research directory that organizes listings by category and helps you compare seller links, prices, sizes, and available quality-control information.

Where do the catalog listings come from?

The items and reference prices on this independent guide come from HipobuyFind. The main buttons open HipobuyFind, where you can browse the current catalog.

Where do I find QC photos for my order?

Check the warehouse section of the agent account you used to place the order. Compare any available inspection photos with the variant you ordered and request additional angles if the agent offers that option.
